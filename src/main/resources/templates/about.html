ABOUT THE PLATFORM

Reuniting Campus Property with Confidence

BU Finder is a community-driven web application built to streamline the lost-and-found recovery process across university grounds, hostels, sports complexes, and libraries.

False-Claim Protection

Distinguishing details, serial numbers, and private marks remain confidential so claimants must prove legitimate ownership.

Hierarchical Taxonomy

Categories, dynamic sub-types, brands, and colors enable instant precision filtering matching the original BU Finder taxonomy.

Verified Handover

Reporters and administrators review submitted proof before approving claims and marking records as safely resolved.

Architectural & Technology Stack
