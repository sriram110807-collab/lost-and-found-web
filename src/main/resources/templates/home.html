How SRM Finder Works

A streamlined, 4-step workflow to help reunite lost property with legitimate owners quickly and securely.

1. Post a Report

Submit a report with location, date, category, and an optional photo of the item.

2. Search & Match

Browse through categorized cards with keyword, category, and location filters.

3. Claim Verification

Submit a claim specifying distinguishing details to verify rightful ownership.

4. Safe Handover

Upon approval, coordinate handover via the student center and mark the item resolved.
